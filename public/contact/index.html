Yes. PocketDay features an offline-first architecture. All transactions, budget updates, and savings goal logs are written to your local device memory with sub-5ms UI responsiveness. When your device reconnects to the internet, changes are silently synced with Google Cloud Firestore.
Most budgeting tools rigidly track from the 1st to the 30th/31st of each calendar month. But if your salary arrives on the 5th, 10th, or 15th, that calendar view is out of sync with your reality. PocketDay lets you set your custom financial cycle start date (e.g. 10th to 9th), automatically adapting your budget progress bars and spending summaries.
