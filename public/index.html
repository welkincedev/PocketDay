Built For Real Life

Everything you need to master your daily finances

PocketDay eliminates complexity by focusing on what truly matters: clear cash flow, realistic budgeting cycles, and total privacy.

Real-Time Income & Expense Tracking

Log transactions in seconds with intuitive categories, notes, payment types, and instant balance calculations. No waiting for network confirmation.

Custom Pay-Cycle Budgeting

Not everyone is paid on the 1st of the month. PocketDay lets you set your financial month to match your salary date (e.g. 10th to 9th) across all reports.

Category-Specific Budget Limits

Set spending limits for Dining, Shopping, Utilities, and Groceries. Track remaining allowance and visual progress bars before overspending occurs.

Savings Goals Tracker

Create dedicated targets with target dates and amounts. Link income transactions directly to your goals and watch your funds grow.

Recurring Subscriptions

Keep recurring Netflix, Spotify, gym, or software renewals in check with automated billing cycle detection and payment generation.

Private On-Device Reminders

Configure daily spending check-in reminders and budget notifications that run purely on your device's native alarm manager without cloud profiling.

Quick Start

How PocketDay works in 3 simple steps

Record Daily Activity

Quickly record an expense or income with an intuitive amount keypad, category picker, and optional notes. Works seamlessly offline.
