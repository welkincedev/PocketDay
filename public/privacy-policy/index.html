Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • Last Updated: November 16, 2026 • Google Play Compliant
Privacy Summary: PocketDay is an offline-first personal finance tracking and budgeting application. We do not connect to your live bank accounts, do not read your SMS messages, and never sell your personal information to third-party advertisers. All cloud data is protected by strict Google Cloud Firestore UID security isolation.

1. Introduction

Welcome to PocketDay ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains in clear, plain language how our mobile application and web services collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the PocketDay application.

By downloading, accessing, or using PocketDay, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect

PocketDay only collects the data necessary to provide you with secure personal finance management, synchronization, and budgeting insights:

A. Account and Identity Information

  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier and for secure authentication.
  • User Identifier (UID): An encrypted, random alphanumeric string assigned by Firebase Authentication to isolate your database documents.
  • Profile Information: Optional display name and avatar photo URL if you sign in using Google Sign-In.

B. Financial Records (Manually Entered by You)

  • Transactions: Transaction amounts, transaction titles, dates, categories (e.g. Food, Shopping, Transport, Utilities), payment methods (e.g. Cash, Card, UPI), and optional personal notes.
  • Budget Plans: Monthly budget caps, category-specific limits, and your custom financial month start date (e.g. 10th of each month).
  • Savings Goals: Goal names, target amounts, target dates, and accumulated balances.
  • Subscriptions: Title, recurring amount, billing cycle (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly), and next payment due date.

C. Local Device Preferences

  • Local Reminder Preferences: Reminder toggle states, daily notification reminder time (hour and minute), stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences.
  • Display Preferences: Dark mode / Light mode UI theme choice.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

To ensure total privacy and security, PocketDay explicitly avoids collecting sensitive financial or surveillance data:

  • NO Bank Credentials or Account Numbers: PocketDay is a manual tracking and budgeting manager. We never ask for or store your bank account numbers, debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, net-banking passwords, or PINs.
  • NO SMS or Call Log Access: PocketDay does not request permissions to read your SMS text messages, OTPs, or phone call logs.
  • NO Background Location Tracking: PocketDay does not track or store your geographic GPS coordinates.
  • NO Advertising Identifiers (AAID/IDFA): PocketDay contains no advertising SDKs and does not profile you across third-party websites or applications.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate application functionality:

  • To authenticate your identity across your devices.
  • To calculate your real-time total balance, monthly income, monthly expenses, category pace, and remaining budget allowance.
  • To support your custom financial month pay cycles.
  • To trigger on-device notifications and spending reminders scheduled by you.
  • To allow permanent account and data deletion upon your request.

5. Where and How Your Information Is Stored

PocketDay uses a hybrid architecture designed for speed and reliability:

  • Local Device Cache: When you record a transaction, it is written immediately to your device's local cache for instant sub-5ms UI updates and complete offline usability.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: When connectivity is present, records are synchronized with Google Cloud Firestore under the Firebase project pocketday-1679a located in Google Cloud data centers.

6. Firebase & Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on trusted enterprise-grade infrastructure providers to maintain reliability and security:

Provider / SDK Purpose Data Processed
Google Firebase Authentication User registration, login, and secure token issuance. Email, password hash, UID, Google OAuth token.
Google Cloud Firestore NoSQL encrypted cloud database storage and sync. User transactions, budgets, goals, subscriptions.
Google Sign-In SDK Optional one-tap OAuth login. Display name, email address, profile photo.
Google Fonts Typography rendering. Font asset delivery (no personal data tracked).

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data or financial logs with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Data is only shared under the following rare circumstances:

  • With Service Providers: Google Cloud Platform hosts our cloud database under confidentiality agreements and strict data processing addendums.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by valid court order, subpoena, or applicable law.

8. Data Security & Multi-Tenant Isolation

We implement robust technical and organizational security controls:

  • Firestore Security Rules: Every database read and write requires strict per-user UID isolation: 
    match /users/{userId}/{document=**} {
  allow read, write: if request.auth != null && request.auth.uid == userId;
}
    No user can access, query, or modify another user's financial records.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your mobile device, the web portal, and Firebase servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud Firestore documents are encrypted on disk using 256-bit Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256).

9. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your information only as long as you maintain an active PocketDay account. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated records at any time.

You can initiate deletion through two straightforward methods:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open PocketDay → Settings/Profile → Tap Delete Account.
  2. Official Web Deletion Portal: Visit our publicly accessible deletion portal at https://pocketday-1679a.web.app/delete-account without needing the app installed.

Upon confirmation, all transactions, budgets, savings goals, recurring subscriptions, profile records, and your authentication credentials are permanently purged from Google Cloud servers.

10. Children's Privacy

PocketDay is designed for individuals of legal age to manage personal finances. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If we become aware that a child under 13 has registered without parental consent, we will promptly delete the account.
